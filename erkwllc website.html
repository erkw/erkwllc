About

We buy inventory. We don't undercut it.

ERKW LLC is a wholesale distribution company operating exclusively in e-commerce. We source products direct from brands and manufacturers, take ownership of the inventory, and sell it through established marketplace channels under our own retail accounts.

Most brands don't have a problem finding buyers online. They have a problem with what happens after the sale — advertised pricing drifting below policy, listings drifting away from approved copy and imagery, and sellers who buy once and disappear. We built our operation around not being that partner.

We are a straightforward wholesale buyer: we place purchase orders, pay on agreed terms, and reorder predictably on the products that move.

Capabilities

What we handle after the purchase order

Distribution is the easy part. These are the operational commitments that protect a brand's channel.

MAP & pricing policy

We price to your published minimum advertised price on every channel, and we do not use pricing automation that can drift below policy.

Listing integrity

Approved titles, bullet copy, imagery and A+ assets used as supplied. No unauthorized variations, bundles, or repackaging.

Demand planning

We track velocity and seasonality on the SKUs we carry and place reorders on a predictable cadence rather than opportunistically.

Channel management

Inventory monitoring, buy-box health, stock-out prevention, and prompt handling of listing suppressions or category issues.

Customer service

We answer marketplace questions, handle returns, and manage reviews on the listings we operate — so the brand isn't fielding them.

Authorization & compliance

We operate only with written authorization, maintain accurate reseller records, and support brand-registry and gating requirements.

For Brands

What a wholesale account with us looks like

We keep onboarding simple and the terms plain. There is no pitch deck and no long negotiation — you send a price list, we send a purchase order.

  • Application. You receive our company packet — LLC documentation, EIN, resale certificate, and channel information.
  • Authorization. We countersign your reseller agreement, MAP policy, and any channel restrictions before the first order.
  • Opening order. First purchase order is prepaid. We test a focused SKU set rather than over-ordering the catalog.
  • Reorder cadence. Once velocity is established, we move to a regular reorder schedule and can discuss standard terms.
  • Reporting. We share sell-through and channel feedback on request — including unauthorized-seller activity we observe on your listings.

Policies

Terms, shipping & privacy

Shipping & Receiving

ERKW LLC provides its designated receiving address to suppliers at the time a purchase order is issued. We accept small parcel and LTL freight and can supply routing instructions, appointment requirements, and receiving contact details on request. Outbound orders placed by retail customers on our marketplace channels ship under the fulfillment and delivery terms published by the applicable marketplace.

Returns

Retail customer returns are administered under the return policies of the marketplace on which the order was placed. Wholesale purchases are subject to the terms of the individual supplier agreement; damaged, defective, or short shipments are reported to the supplier within ten business days of receipt with supporting documentation.

Pricing & MAP Compliance

ERKW LLC honors the minimum advertised price policy of each brand we are authorized to sell. We do not employ repricing software configured to price below a supplier's stated MAP floor. Suspected policy violations by ERKW LLC on any channel should be reported to sales@erkwllc.com and will be corrected promptly.
